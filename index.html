What is a UUID?

A Universally Unique Identifier is a 128-bit value commonly used to identify records, objects, and sessions without a central numbering system.

02 /

Why UUID v4?

Version 4 UUIDs are based on randomness. They are useful when independently generated identifiers need an extremely low risk of collision.

03 /

Is it secure?

This tool uses crypto.randomUUID() when available, with a secure crypto.getRandomValues() fallback. UUIDs are identifiers, not passwords or secrets.

Frequently asked questions

Can two UUID v4 values be the same?

In theory, yes. But with 122 random bits, accidental collisions are extraordinarily unlikely when UUIDs are generated correctly.

Do UUIDs include personal information?

Random v4 UUIDs do not encode your name, device address, or creation time.

Can I use these UUIDs commercially?

Yes. You can use the generated UUIDs in personal or commercial applications at no cost.

Can I generate UUIDs offline?

Yes. Once the site is loaded, generation works in your browser without a network request.
